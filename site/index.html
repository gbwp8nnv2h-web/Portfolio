AI-Enabled Learning Design Workflow

I use ChatGPT and Claude to accelerate research, synthesis, scenario development, first-pass learning design, content drafting, workflow planning, and quality review—while keeping human judgment responsible for accuracy, context, privacy, audience fit, and final instructional quality.

1. Start with performance

Define the real work behavior and what successful performance looks like.

2. Accelerate thoughtfully

Use AI for research, alternatives, examples, scenarios, and first-pass structures.

3. Verify and design

Human-review claims, sequence practice, add feedback, and build support for transfer.

Design Principles

Begin with performance

Design toward what a learner needs to do differently after the experience.

Make complexity usable

Translate unfamiliar material into clear structures, examples, scenarios, and decisions.

Use evidence to iterate

Let assessments, behavior, platform data, and feedback drive redesign.
